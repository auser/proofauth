# Request and presentation canonicalization vectors

These vectors use the starter identity, policy, and request generated by
`proofauth generate-example`. The deterministic issuer and subject signing
keys contain 32 bytes of `0x01` and `0x02`, respectively, and the issuer epoch
is `7`.

`request-canonical.json` is the exact JCS encoding of the request. Its
domain-separated `proofauth/request/v1` digest is:

```text
eb2b2774be9aa3e24e5722df7cc8188f2f648b9bd6ba7c1e23ad26493d2be500
```

`presentation-canonical.json` is the exact JCS encoding of the deterministic,
dual-signed presentation. Its domain-separated `proofauth/presentation/v1`
digest, including both signatures, is:

```text
9a5bf931116a19ab007334eb1a9374153580830046d30ce11fcd7dc4cfaf1be2
```

The identity root and policy hash in the presentation are the raw SHA-256
digest bytes from their official `uor-addr` addresses.
